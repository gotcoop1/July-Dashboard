# 2026 Budget Source Files

Which finance files feed the dashboards, and which parts to trust.

## Files
| File | Used for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `07 2026 Budget v Actual Dashboard Overview & by Ministry.xlsx` | YTD actual and YTD budget, Jan–Jul 2026 | High-level ministry totals only. No transaction detail. |
| `Final_2026_Budget_.xlsx`, "Finance Team Summary" tab | Annual budget (board-approved) | Authoritative. Ties to its own grand total of $6,470,085.22. |
| `Final_2026_Budget_.xlsx`, "Original Expenses" tab | Not used | Totals differ from Finance Team Summary |
| `Final_2026_Budget_.xlsx`, "Revised Expenses" tab | Not used | Totals differ from both other tabs |

## Things to know
- "Capital Savings (Debt Retirement)" ($837,591) is in the annual budget but isn't tracked at the ministry level.
- "Total KOTK Expenses" ($150,883.57) and "Total KOTK Income" ($169,975.00) sit under "Other Revenue/Expenditures" and are excluded from ministry totals.
- Label mismatch: "Staff Support Ministry" in the tracking workbook = "Staff Cont Ed/Conf & Min Exp" in the budget file (provisional).
